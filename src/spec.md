# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Remove scroll animations from the hero section and change the background to white for better readability.

**Planned changes:**
- Remove any scroll-based parallax or animation effects from the homepage hero section
- Change the hero section background from the current image to a white/blank background
- Ensure all text in the hero section has sufficient contrast and readability

**User-visible outcome:** The hero section will display with a clean white background and static content that doesn't animate on scroll, making all text more readable.
