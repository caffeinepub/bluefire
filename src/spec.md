# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Create a professional Spanish-language presentation website for BlueFire, a cybersecurity consulting company targeting the Mexican market.

**Planned changes:**
- Hero section with company name, tagline, and CTA button with background image
- Services section explaining four core offerings: Registro de Activos, Evaluación de Vulnerabilidades, Remediación de Vulnerabilidades, and Segmentación de Redes
- Market Context section with Mexican cybersecurity statistics and threat landscape data
- Target Clients section differentiating PYMEs vs Maquiladoras service approaches
- Workflow Overview section explaining MAIVOR methodology for IT/OT environments
- Pilot Program section promoting quick scan service for 1-3 IPs/domains
- Contact section with Angel Medina's details and contact form
- Professional cybersecurity visual design theme throughout
- All content and UI elements in Spanish

**User-visible outcome:** A complete single-page Spanish website showcasing BlueFire's cybersecurity consulting services, market expertise, and contact information for Mexican businesses seeking protection against cyber threats.
